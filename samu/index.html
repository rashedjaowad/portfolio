The Daily Video Deadline Check

Every weekday morning it reads the video tracker, spots videos that are overdue, due soon or stuck in a stage, asks the editor for a status, and sends you one summary with draft client updates to review.

You stay in the loop

Client updates are drafts for a human to review. Your rule, "talk to me before telling clients about problems," is built in.

Editors get a nudge

A short, friendly message asks for a status and an ETA, and invites them to say what they are blocked on.

One summary, not noise

All flagged videos land in a single message, sorted by how urgent they are.
